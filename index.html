Organisation Context

Diksiyon ve İletişim Akademi

Diksiyon ve İletişim Akademi is a Türkiye-based digital education organisation led by Ramazan Yıldız and Tolga Günhan, two widely recognised Turkish communication trainers and social media creators. The organisation specialises in diction (clear speech, pronunciation, articulation and vocal delivery), confident speaking, public speaking and practical communication skills.

The Academy operates as the umbrella organisation behind its training services, content channels and digital education products. Ramazan Yıldız and Tolga Günhan reach their wider audience primarily through @diksiyonsayfasi, which currently has more than 750,000 Instagram followers and over 50,000 YouTube subscribers.

The Instagram account functions as the organisation's principal educational content and audience channel. Instructor-led online programmes are marketed and sold through the Academy's main website.

The wider digital education structure includes:

Instructor-led Online Training

Live online diction and communication programmes delivered by the Academy's instructors and sold through its main website.

Yirmibirgün

A structured 21-day digital training programme delivered through pre-recorded lessons covering diction, voice and communication.

Lernbee

The international, multi-tenant extension of the digital education model. Lernbee adapts the existing programme, content, pricing, positioning and customer journey for audiences outside Türkiye.

Case 02 · 7 May 2024 to November 2025

Yirmibirgün: Live Webinar Sales System

Commercial operations, team coordination and system execution.

Context

Yirmibirgün was launched on 7 May 2024 as a structured 21-day digital training programme built around pre-recorded lessons on diction (clear speech, pronunciation, articulation and vocal delivery), voice and communication.

The product concept and initial sales model were developed by management with support from an external marketing team. The original system used ClickFunnels and a webinar-funnel approach popularised by Russell Brunson. The original product concept, initial webinar model and selection of the sales methodology are not attributed solely to Mehmet.

Operating Model

Prospective customers entered the system through paid advertising and registered for live online seminars held on selected days each week. Ramazan Yıldız and Tolga Günhan delivered educational content during the seminars and presented the Yirmibirgün programme to participants. The commercial journey connected paid advertising, seminar registration, attendance, programme presentation, customer communication, payment and student onboarding.

Paid Advertisement→ Registration→ Live Seminar→ Programme Offer→ Customer Communication→ Payment→ Student Onboarding

Responsibilities

  • Coordinated the recurring live-seminar sales operation
  • Managed teams involved in customer communication and sales
  • Monitored the journey from registration to completed purchase
  • Organised customer follow-up and programme enrolment
  • Connected advertising, seminar and sales processes
  • Converted management decisions into operational tasks and workflows
  • Assigned responsibilities and monitored execution
  • Responded to operational problems during high-volume sales periods
  • Reviewed registration, attendance, sales and cost data
  • Supported changes to audiences, offers, communication and workflows
  • Maintained the repeatability of the weekly seminar operation

The system's original concept was not developed as an individual initiative. Responsibility centred on operating the model at scale, coordinating the teams involved, implementing management decisions and controlling how the process functioned in practice.

Performance Management

Performance was assessed across the full commercial journey rather than through advertising activity alone. Registration volume, seminar attendance, completed sales, cost per sale and customer feedback were considered together. When results changed, responses could involve advertising audiences, creative materials, seminar scheduling, customer follow-up, sales communication or the programme offer.

Acquire→ Measure→ Diagnose→ Change→ Reallocate

Back into Measure, continuously. The 25 April seminar (403 attendees, 1 sale) is a performance-diagnosis example: caught immediately through this monitoring, early enough to act on.

Limitations

The model produced significant sales but became increasingly difficult to sustain. Each cycle depended on recurring live events, instructor availability, operational preparation and continuous coordination across the supporting teams.

Brand perception also became a concern. The seminars were promoted as free educational events but concluded with an offer for a paid programme. Some participants reacted negatively because the commercial purpose was not always clear before attendance.

Profitability gradually declined as the operational demands of the model continued. These limitations created the requirement for an always-on sales structure that did not depend on repeated live seminars.

Understands paid acquisition and what happens after the click, at organisational scale.

Case 03 · November 2025 to Present

Yirmibirgün: Evergreen VSL Sales System

Commercial model development, paid acquisition and creative management.

Context

The live webinar system had produced significant sales but had become operationally demanding, less profitable and increasingly difficult to sustain. The new requirement was an always-on commercial journey that could operate without recurring live presentations.

Operating Model

The proposal centred on converting the presentation structure already used in the live seminars into a VSL (Video Sales Letter). The strongest elements of the seminar's sales narrative could therefore be retained while removing the dependency on scheduled events. The VSL funnel concept originated from Mehmet Cihangir Yırgal. The final decision to replace the live webinar model was collaborative and was not made independently by one person.

Paid advertisements direct prospective customers to a dedicated landing page. Visitors can watch the VSL, review the programme offer, complete payment and receive access without attending a scheduled seminar.

Paid Advertisement→Landing Page→VSL→Programme Offer→Checkout→Immediate Access

For comparison, the live-event model it replaced:

Live Seminar→Registration→Scheduled Event→Live Presentation→Purchase

Responsibilities

  • Proposed the conversion of the seminar presentation into a VSL
  • Participated in the commercial decision-making process
  • Contributed to the structure of the new customer journey
  • Adapted the seminar's presentation logic for an on-demand format
  • Supported the landing-page and offer structure
  • Converted commercial requirements into technical briefs
  • Coordinated requirements with the development team
  • Managed a significant part of the paid advertising operation
  • Planned and managed advertising creatives
  • Monitored funnel conversion and ROAS
  • Coordinated performance-led changes to creatives and funnel elements
  • Supported the continued operation of the system

Scope

Engineering delivery remained with the development team. WooCommerce checkout, TutorLMS delivery and later development work are not presented as individually engineered by Mehmet. Responsibility centred on the connective layer between commercial requirements, advertising decisions, creative management, funnel structure and technical implementation.

Case 04 · Internationalisation · Market Entry · Product Localisation

Lernbee: Multi-tenant International Education Platform

Technical architecture, product localisation and international execution.

Context

The decision to take the Yirmibirgün education model into international markets originated from the management team. The objective was to make the existing digital programme available outside Türkiye through a separate international brand.

Yirmibirgün already operated through a relatively simple digital learning infrastructure. Rather than building a completely separate platform for every market, a multi-tenant model was proposed.

Operating Model

The multi-tenant concept, technical structure and implementation were developed under Mehmet Cihangir Yırgal's responsibility. The shared architecture allows Yirmibirgün and Lernbee to operate through the same underlying technical infrastructure while presenting separate market experiences. Users entering from Türkiye are presented with the Yirmibirgün brand, while international users are presented with Lernbee. The shared system supports different languages, positioning, pricing, creative materials and customer journeys without requiring an independent codebase for every market.

Türkiye
  • Yirmibirgün brand
  • Turkish content
  • Domestic pricing and positioning
  • Domestic customer journey
International Markets
  • Lernbee brand
  • English, Malay and Arabic content
  • Market-specific pricing and positioning
  • Localised customer journeys

Scope

International delivery required more than website translation. The product, content, pricing, creative materials, calls to action and customer journey required adaptation for their intended markets. Selected videos from the original Yirmibirgün programme were re-recorded with a presentation style suitable for international audiences. Existing advertising materials that had performed effectively in Türkiye were translated and adapted through HeyGen. Additional translations and market-specific creative materials were also produced.
