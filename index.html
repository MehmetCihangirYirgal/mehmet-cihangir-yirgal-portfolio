Case 02 · 7 May 2024 to November 2025

Yirmibirgün: Live Webinar Sales System

Commercial operations, team coordination and system execution.

Context

Yirmibirgün was launched on 7 May 2024 as a structured 21-day digital training programme built around pre-recorded lessons on diction, voice and communication, using ClickFunnels and a webinar-funnel approach popularised by Russell Brunson.

Operating Model

Prospective customers entered the system through paid advertising and registered for live online seminars held on selected days each week. Ramazan Yıldız and Tolga Günhan delivered educational content during the seminars and presented the Yirmibirgün programme to participants. The commercial journey connected paid advertising, seminar registration, attendance, programme presentation, customer communication, payment and student onboarding.

Paid Advertisement→ Registration→ Live Seminar→ Programme Offer→ Customer Communication→ Payment→ Student Onboarding

Responsibilities

Role and ownership: the product concept and initial webinar model were developed by management with an external marketing team. My responsibility was to coordinate acquisition, webinar delivery, customer communication, sales and enrolment as one scalable operating system.

  • Monitored registration, attendance, sales and cost-per-sale data
  • Diagnosed performance problems as they emerged
  • Directed changes to creative, targeting, follow-up, offer and operations
  • Kept the weekly sales cycle running reliably at high volume

Performance Management

Performance was assessed across the full commercial journey rather than through advertising activity alone. Registration volume, seminar attendance, completed sales, cost per sale and customer feedback were considered together. When results changed, responses could involve advertising audiences, creative materials, seminar scheduling, customer follow-up, sales communication or the programme offer.

Acquire→ Measure→ Diagnose→ Change→ Reallocate

Back into Measure, continuously. The 25 April seminar (403 attendees, 1 sale) is a performance-diagnosis example: caught immediately through this monitoring, early enough to act on.

Limitations

The model produced strong sales but became harder to sustain — dependent on recurring live events, instructor availability and constant team coordination, with profitability declining as the operational load grew. Some participants also reacted to the free-to-paid seminar structure, since the commercial intent wasn't always clear beforehand. These pressures led to the always-on model in the next case.

I managed ad performance across the full journey to a sale, not just cost per click.

Case 03 · November 2025 to Present

Yirmibirgün: Evergreen VSL Sales System

Commercial model development, paid acquisition and creative management.

Context

The live webinar system had produced significant sales but had become operationally demanding, less profitable and increasingly difficult to sustain. The new requirement was an always-on commercial journey that could operate without recurring live presentations.

Operating Model

The proposal centred on converting the presentation structure already used in the live seminars into a VSL (Video Sales Letter). The strongest elements of the seminar's sales narrative could therefore be retained while removing the dependency on scheduled events. The VSL funnel concept originated from Mehmet Cihangir Yırgal. The final decision to replace the live webinar model was collaborative and was not made independently by one person.

Paid advertisements direct prospective customers to a dedicated landing page. Visitors can watch the VSL, review the programme offer, complete payment and receive access without attending a scheduled seminar.

Paid Advertisement→Landing Page→VSL→Programme Offer→Checkout→Immediate Access

For comparison, the live-event model it replaced:

Live Seminar→Registration→Scheduled Event→Live Presentation→Purchase

Responsibilities

  • Proposed converting the seminar into a VSL and helped shape the new customer journey
  • Designed the landing-page and offer structure
  • Translated commercial requirements into technical briefs for the development team
  • Managed the paid advertising operation and its creative output
  • Monitored funnel conversion and ROAS, directing performance-led changes
  • Kept the system running day to day

Scope

Engineering delivery remained with the development team throughout — the original WooCommerce/TutorLMS checkout has since been replaced by a Next.js-based platform, which I also did not personally build. Responsibility centred on the connective layer between commercial requirements, advertising decisions, creative management, funnel structure and technical implementation.

Case 04 · Internationalisation · Market Entry · Product Localisation

Lernbee: Multi-tenant International Education Platform

Technical architecture, product localisation and international execution.

Context

The decision to take the Yirmibirgün education model into international markets originated from the management team. The objective was to make the existing digital programme available outside Türkiye through a separate international brand.

Yirmibirgün already operated through a relatively simple digital learning infrastructure. Rather than building a completely separate platform for every market, a multi-tenant model was proposed.

Operating Model

The multi-tenant concept, technical structure and implementation were developed under Mehmet Cihangir Yırgal's responsibility. The shared architecture allows Yirmibirgün and Lernbee to operate through the same underlying technical infrastructure while presenting separate market experiences. Users entering from Türkiye are presented with the Yirmibirgün brand, while international users are presented with Lernbee. The shared system supports different languages, positioning, pricing, creative materials and customer journeys without requiring an independent codebase for every market.

Türkiye
  • Yirmibirgün brand
  • Turkish content
  • Domestic pricing and positioning
  • Domestic customer journey
International Markets
  • Lernbee brand
  • English, Malay and Arabic content
  • Market-specific pricing and positioning
  • Localised customer journeys

Scope

International delivery required more than website translation. The product, content, pricing, creative materials, calls to action and customer journey required adaptation for their intended markets. Selected videos from the original Yirmibirgün programme were re-recorded with a presentation style suitable for international audiences. Existing advertising materials that had performed effectively in Türkiye were translated and adapted through HeyGen. Additional translations and market-specific creative materials were also produced.

  • Proposed the multi-tenant platform model
  • Designed and implemented the technical structure connecting Yirmibirgün and Lernbee
  • Separated the domestic and international brand experiences on the same shared infrastructure
  • Coordinated product adaptation and localisation for international markets

Commercial Outcome

Lernbee recorded its first international sales during its launch month. These initial purchases demonstrated that the shared platform could present, sell and deliver the programme outside Türkiye through the Lernbee brand. Lernbee is not presented as fully scaled at this stage.

I adapted an existing product for a genuinely new market — not translated it for one.
